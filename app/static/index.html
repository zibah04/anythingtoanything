JUST BETWEEN YOU AND YOUR FILES

A little privacy, by design.

On-device conversions

Common image, text, and CSV/JSON conversions happen in your browser. Those files are never uploaded. Look for the “On device” label.

Server conversions

Complex formats are sent to this app’s server. Each conversion uses a temporary folder that is removed when processing finishes or fails. Converted files are returned directly, with no stored download links.

What stays in the browser

Your original files and download results stay in this tab’s memory until you remove them, clear the queue, or close the page. We use no accounts, analytics, or cookies.

A practical limit

Files are limited to 25 MB. Password-protected files and scanned PDF OCR are not supported. For sensitive documents, use a deployment you trust.
